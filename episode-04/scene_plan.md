# Episode 4: "How to read drum music" — scene plan

Times are DRAFT estimates from the script (no narration yet, ~4:40). They will shift once the real narration is timed; the scenes follow the words automatically.
Look: same as Episodes 2-3 (blue rays, mascot bottom-left, karaoke captions, "EPISODE 4" progress bar with stars, gift box for the bonus). Reuses the Episode 3 kit, sticks (approved upper-right placement), and beat chart.

| # | Scene | What's on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | Kit plays the rock beat with the Episode 3 beat chart; on "secret code" the chart turns into real drum notation. "READ YOUR FIRST BEAT!" title; gift box + "BONUS: SPOT THE MISTAKE!". |
| 2 | Recap of Lesson 3 | Kit + chart, rows light up as named (hi-hat / kick / snare); "WATCH LESSON 3 FIRST!" card; "LET'S CRACK THE CODE!". |
| 3 | The staff | "DRUM" letters → staff; the 5 lines draw in one by one, counted 1-5 in pink; the 4 spaces glow; birds sit on lines and in spaces; "WHERE IT SITS = WHAT TO PLAY". |
| 4 | Clef, 4/4, bars | Zoom on the drum clef ("DRUMS ONLY!" door sign), then the two fours ("= 4 COUNTS"), then bar lines; count cursor runs through one bar. |
| 5 | Where each drum lives | Piano ("which key") vs kit ("which drum"); "LOW SOUNDS SIT LOW, HIGH SOUNDS SIT HIGH"; kick note pops into the bottom space, snare into the middle (3rd) space, hi-hat x above the staff, each with its character + label + BOOM!/CRACK!/TICK!; "CYMBALS = METAL = X"; "THAT'S YOUR WHOLE MAP!". |
| 6 | What a note looks like | Big note: HEAD, then STEM; x-head for cymbals; a beamed pair labelled "1 &" ("PAIR = A COUNT + ITS AND"); "NAMES COMING IN A FUTURE LESSON!". |
| 7 | Build the beat | Staff fills in layer by layer: hi-hat x's (cursor follows the spoken count), kick, snare; "OUR ROCK BEAT, WRITTEN DOWN!". |
| 8 | How to read it | Column highlight straight down each count: 1 = HI-HAT + KICK, & = JUST HI-HAT, 2 = HI-HAT + SNARE. |
| 9 | Read + play | Kit plays along while the cursor reads the staff; big count-in; "YOU JUST READ YOUR FIRST BEAT!" + confetti. |
| 10 | Recap | "SAY THE MAP WITH ME!": kick / snare / hi-hat notes pop in with BOTTOM / MIDDLE / X ON TOP. |
| 11 | Bonus: spot the mistake | Gift opens; staff with the snare wrongly on 1 and 3 (red), magnifying glass, pause button; reveal: red ✗, then the snare moves to 2 and 4, ✓ + confetti. |
| 12 | Outro | "YOU CAN READ DRUM MUSIC!", next episode card "ADD MORE TO YOUR BEAT!", subscribe. |

Notation used (standard drum notation): percussion clef, 4/4, kick = bottom space (stem down), snare = 3rd space, hi-hat = x above the top line; hi-hat/snare stems up with beams in pairs.
