## Description

Boom, tick, crack, tick... that's a rock beat, and in 5 minutes YOU are going to play it! 🥁
In this fun lesson for kids AND adults, we build the most famous drum beat in the world one layer at a time: hi-hat, then kick, then snare. Count along, play it slow, then play it at real rock speed. Stick around to the end for a bonus trick that lets you practice anywhere, even without a drum kit!

• Count like a drummer: 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &
• Right hand: hi-hat on EVERY count
• Right foot: kick on 1 and 3
• Left hand: snare on 2 and 4
• Why your arms cross (it's totally normal!)
• How to fix rushing and "not together"
• Lap drumming: practice with no drum kit
